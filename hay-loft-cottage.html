About the cottage

Beams, character, and a bed that suits you

The Hayloft takes its name from its former life storing the farm's hay, and it still wears that history proudly in its exposed beams and cosy proportions. The bed can be made up as a super king or as twin beds, just tell us your preference when you book. The seating area has a comfortable sofa bed for a third person. There is a Smart TV and WiFi.

The kitchen/diner is well equipped for cooking. There is a spacious shower room with loo. Central heating keeps things comfortable whatever the season, and there's ample off-road parking right outside.

Like Kingfisher, The Hayloft shares access to the large garden and the heated outdoor pool, open from mid-May to mid-September, so however you like to spend your days, it's all close at hand.
