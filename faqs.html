FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything guests most often ask us before, during and after their stay. Can't find what you need? Just get in touch.

We, the owners, live on site in the adjacent farmhouse. If you have any questions during your stay we will be happy to help.

There is designated parking for two vehicles for each cottage.

Yes, high-quality toiletries and linen are included. Guests are provided with a fresh set of towels. Pool towels are also included.

Our property is a working farm with deep water features and heavy machinery. For peace of mind and child safety, we can only accept non-walking infants (under 12 months) and adults.

Yes. We can provide grazing but you need to bring your own corral kit. There are no stables at the farm. Contact us directly for more information if you want to bring your horse.

Due to the resident animals and birds we regret we are not able to accept dogs or other pets.

Yes, there is wireless broadband in both cottages, but provision is subject to network availability and cannot be guaranteed. The WiFi may not extend to all rooms in the cottages.

There is a spacious games room with a wide selection of puzzles, games and DVDs. The room has underfloor heating, a games console and a large screen Smart TV.

There are several shops including a Co-op supermarket 10 minutes' drive away in Cleobury Mortimer. The Co-op also has a petrol station and car charging points, and there are a number of pubs and restaurants in the town.

Both cottages are non-smoking and non-vaping.

The pool is usually open from mid-May to mid-September. It is shared by both cottages and use of the pool is unsupervised. Whilst every care has been taken to make the swimming pool areas as safe as possible, all facilities are used at your own risk.

For week long stays arrival time is from 4pm and departure is by 11am. For weekend breaks arrival is from mid-day Friday and departure by 6pm Sunday.
